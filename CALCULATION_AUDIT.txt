Ring configurator calculation audit — 2026-10-14

RESULT
The numerical checks pass after correcting stone spacing. This validates the
implemented design model, not the suitability of a ring for manufacture.

FIXED
Stone spacing used arc lengths on the outer band radius, ignoring the lower
radius of embedded stone girdles. EU 48, width 10 mm, nominal thickness 1 mm,
200% dome, a 5 mm main stone and 3 mm sides in a channel produced an actual
stone-section intersection of approximately 0.01354 mm.
Spacing now accounts for each stone's angular footprint at its seated height.
The channel endpoint uses the same footprint. Pavé receives the same correction.
The reproduced intersecting pair now has approximately 0.13635 mm separation
along a separating axis. This is a geometry result, not a setting tolerance.

CHECKED CALCULATIONS
1. Ring sizing: inner radius = EU circumference / (2*pi); diameter = size/pi.
   EU 54 = 17.18873385 mm, displayed as 17.19 mm. Comfort fit opens towards the
   edges by up to 0.12 mm in radius; the centre retains the selected bore size.
2. Band: base radius = inner radius + nominal thickness. Outer profiles and
   additional dome change the local thickness. Dome above 100% adds up to
   min(2.1 mm, 0.28*width) at the centre. At width 6 mm, 200% dome adds 1.68 mm.
   The percentage is a design control, not a percentage increase in thickness.
3. Patterns: non-negative radial recesses, edge fading, and seam continuity
   checked for every pattern. Circumferential repetition is rounded to an
   integer, so spacing is nominal. Diagonal spacing is measured in the model's
   axial/circumferential coordinates, not perpendicular to the groove.
   Recesses are capped when the model's 0.12 mm wall floor would be exceeded.
4. Stone sizes: width allowance is 0.8 mm total; size is also capped by seat
   depth, then rounded DOWN to 0.1 mm. Main maximum 5 mm; side maximum 3 mm.
   Round size denotes diameter; square size denotes side length.
5. Positions: symmetric left/right placement, separate main and side sizes,
   0/2/4 side stones, fixed 15-stone Pavé arrangement, and colour assignments
   agree with the configuration and exports. No-stone mode has no stone cuts.
6. Stone depth: preview crown = 0.16*size, pavilion = 0.43*size. Size limits
   preserve a nominal 0.08 mm clearance at the tip above the central bore.
7. Carats: round = size^2 * (0.62*size) * 0.0061 * density/3.52;
   square/Princess = size^2 * (0.70*size) * 0.0083 * density/3.52.
   Factors match published diamond weight approximations. Density scaling
   accounts for actual approximate mass rather than diamond-equivalent mass.
   Representative densities: diamond 3.52, moissanite 3.22, corundum 4.00,
   spinel 3.60, beryl 2.72, tsavorite 3.61.
   5 mm round diamond: 0.47275 ct -> 0.47 ct.
   5 mm square diamond: 0.72625 ct -> 0.73 ct.
   5 mm round sapphire: 0.5372159 ct -> 0.54 ct.
   3 mm round diamond: 0.102114 ct -> 0.10 ct.
   Values increase cubically with size and remain positive for small stones.
   Side ranges use the selected active stone types; values are per stone.
8. Stored values: supported numeric ranges, step rounding, invalid values,
   configuration round trips, and exported dimensions/stone counts checked.
9. Rendering math: inspected cylindrical placement, mesh scaling, facet planes,
   profile normals, camera scaling, and optical shader calculations. Materials,
   refraction, colour dispersion, surface textures, and engraving are visual
   approximations; there is no metal mass, price, or engraving-depth estimator.

LIMITS THAT REMAIN
- Carats use assumed depths and representative densities, not measured stones.
  The preview cut has a different simplified depth (59%); the estimate is
  intentionally independent of mesh volume. Real cut proportions, inclusions,
  girdles, and species-specific cuts can change weight substantially.
- The 0.12 mm wall floor and 0.08 mm tip clearance are geometric safeguards,
  not validated manufacturing minima for any of the offered materials.
- Setting construction is illustrative. In particular, a channel uses rails
  sized to its widest stone: substantially smaller side stones do not receive
  a separately narrowed channel. The preview does not verify secure retention,
  seat fit, prong strength, casting tolerances, or all setting-metal collisions.
- Profile dimensions, square setting height, and pattern depths describe the
  parametric model; no manufacturing CAD or physical sample was validated.

VERIFICATION
node --test: 22 passing tests, including:
- 5,832 boundary combinations across nine settings, three sizes, three band
  thicknesses, three widths, three domes, four outer and two inner profiles.
  Checks finite dimensions, stone counts, width limits, tip clearance,
  separated angular footprints, no wraparound, and sampled minimum wall.
- All 15 stone types, both cut assumptions, sizes 0.5 through 5.0 mm.
- Export counts, colours and diameter for all nine settings and side counts.
An additional temporary Three.js r147 mesh audit sampled actual gemstone
vertices in 2,916 configurations. Minimum observed bore clearance 0.082997 mm;
all adjacent angular envelopes separated after the fix. This is sampled
coverage, not an exhaustive proof for every continuous geometric surface.
The regression case was additionally checked using convex cross-section
intersection. Browser smoke test showed the corrected configuration and carat
labels with no captured console warnings/errors. git diff --check passed.
